# Look test

Run this on the PNG (or the variants sheet) after the measured report is clean. Code already counted overlaps and crossings; your eyes judge what numbers cannot. A no about meaning goes to the spec; a no about composition goes to another variant or a hint.

1. **Message.** At a glance, does the reader get the one thing this diagram is for? Would a newcomer know where to start?
2. **One question.** Can you say what this diagram answers in one sentence, and does the title say it?
3. **Entry point.** Does the eye land on the start of the main flow, at the top left or the start of the direction?
4. **Flow.** Does the main path read in one direction without doubling back?
5. **Grouping.** Do the boxes match how the reader thinks about the system (ownership, trust boundary, runtime)?
6. **Truth.** Does every part and wire exist in the sources? Is anything important missing?
7. **Words.** Are all labels one to three words? Is there any sentence on the canvas that belongs in `about`?
8. **Encoding.** Do solid, dashed and dotted wires mean what the spec says (sync, async, data)? Is tone used for at most one category?
9. **Size.** Is it readable at the width it will be seen? If not: change direction, split, or rely on the interactive page.
10. **Shape.** Is the main path straight? Are the gaps even? Does any wire take a detour around the picture or step between boxes that should line up? See "Why a layout looks off" in [layout](layout.md).
11. **Calm.** Is there anything you could remove without losing meaning? Remove it.
